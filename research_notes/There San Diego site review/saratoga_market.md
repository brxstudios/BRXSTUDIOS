# Saratoga Springs, NY: Local Media / City-Guide Competitive Landscape and Market (for HereSaratoga.com)

Research date: 2026-10-19. Method note: web search only. Direct page fetches failed with DNS errors for discoversaratoga.org, saratogaliving.com, mannixmarketing.com, census.gov and assets.simpleviewinc.com (the Discover Saratoga PDFs), so several figures come from search-result summaries of those pages rather than a full read. Those are marked "(via search snippet)" and should be spot-checked before publication.

## Competitors and their offerings (audience, newsletters, social, ad rates)

### Takeaway
The market is fragmented across a tourism bureau (Discover Saratoga, the largest email list at 47,000+), a vendor-run directory site (Saratoga.com, Mannix Marketing), a print magazine that moved its daily coverage to a small Substack (Saratoga Living After Hours, about 4,000+ subscribers), a free weekly paper (Saratoga TODAY), a shrunken Alden-owned daily (The Saratogian), and regional outlets (Times Union, Daily Gazette) where Saratoga is one beat among many. None of them clearly runs a modern, personality-driven weekly "what to do / where to eat" newsletter with Instagram-first distribution, which is the ThereSanDiego model. Almost no local outlet publishes ad rates.

## Market: population, visitors, seasonality, businesses, tourism spending

### Takeaway
Saratoga Springs is a small city (about 28.5–28.7K residents) inside a fast-growing, affluent county (about 240K) and a metro of about 913K. Tourism is large for that size: more than $900M in county visitor spending in 2024, and a racing meet that draws more than 1M paid admissions each summer. That means a concentrated summer audience and advertiser surge, with a weaker off-season.

### Inferences
- Food and beverage is the largest visitor-spending category in the county (about $318M in 2023), which suits a food-led guide well.
- Advertiser demand probably peaks June–Labor Day. Off-season revenue will have to come from locals and the 913K-person metro, such as day-trippers from Albany and Clifton Park.

## Gaps in coverage and local sponsored-content pricing precedent

### Takeaway
Clear openings: (1) a free, modern, weekly "things to do this weekend" newsletter for locals (Saratoga Living suspended its calendar newsletter; Discover Saratoga's list is tourism and member marketing); (2) systematic tracking of new restaurant openings and closings for Saratoga specifically (the TU and Gazette cover the whole region); (3) year-round and off-season content; (4) a Saratoga-specific Instagram brand above about 30K followers. Published local pricing for sponsored content is nearly absent. The only rate found is a regional print benchmark.

### Cited Findings
- Saratoga Living suspended its online Calendar and calendar newsletter until further notice — [Saratoga Living: The Calendar](https://saratogaliving.com/category/the-calendar/page/5/)
- Saratoga Living's daily coverage shrank to a Substack after February 2022 and relies partly on reader payments ($5 a month) — [SLAH explainer](https://saratogaliving.com/saratoga-living-after-hours/)
- All Over Albany, the region's main independent "what's going on" blog, closed in 2018 — [chuckthewriter.blog](https://chuckthewriter.blog/2018/12/21/thanks-for-all-the-good-things-all-over-albany/)
- The Saratogian has been cut back under Alden ownership, with shared staff and a smaller office (2017) — [WAMC](https://www.wamc.org/new-york-news/2017-08-11/saratogian-newspaper-moves-out-of-downtown-building); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Saratogian)
- Restaurant openings and closings are tracked regionally (TU monthly list, Gazette new-restaurants page), not with a Saratoga focus — [TU Jan 2026 list via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/restaurants-opened-closed-january-2026-171636618.html); [Gazette](https://www.dailygazette.com/food/new-capital-region-restaurants/article_1d797d60-c92b-11ef-9dbd-23546afa361a.html)
- Pricing benchmark: Adirondack Sports (regional magazine) charges $825 for a full-page ad at 1–2 insertions (2024 rate card) — [ADK Sports rate card](https://AdkSports.com/s/2024-adk-sports-media-rate-card-15.pdf)
- Paid-subscription benchmark: SLAH charges $5 a month or $50 a year — [SLAH Substack](https://saratogaliving.substack.com)
- Discover Saratoga sells dedicated email blasts to 47K+ and newsletter banners (prices unpublished in the excerpts) — [Discover Saratoga Advertise](https://www.discoversaratoga.org/advertise/)

### Inferences
- The likely price anchor for a HereSaratoga sponsored story or newsletter slot is whatever Discover Saratoga charges for an email blast and Saratoga Living charges for print or digital placements. Neither is public, so a call or a request for a media kit is needed.
- Off-season (November–April) content is the most defensible gap: tourism outlets go quiet, and local businesses need traffic most at that time.

### Gaps
- No Saratoga-specific published price for sponsored articles, Instagram posts or newsletter sponsorships found from any outlet. Influencer rates are on Collabstr profiles but were not extracted.
- Whether Saratoga.com's 20K newsletter is still active, and how often it sends, is unconfirmed.
- I did not verify whether any newer local newsletter (for example a Substack or beehiiv launch in 2025–26) exists. Substack's internal search was not available.
