## 1. What advertising products exist and what is included in each?

### Takeaway
ThereSanDiego sells a menu of self-serve local ad products from landing pages on a funnel subdomain. The products are sponsored stories (written articles promoted with paid Facebook and Instagram ads), newsletter placements and dedicated emails, Instagram/Facebook social posts (native, promo-offer and giveaway formats), deal listings, event "boosts" and a one-time business profile listing. Restaurant-specific bundles appear on an older page.

### Cited Findings
- **Sponsored Stories:** the team writes an article from an interview or from information the client provides, publishes it on the site and promotes it "with targeted Facebook and Instagram ads to make sure it gets real, measurable visibility." Tiers carry guaranteed monthly impressions, and campaign management and organic impressions are not charged. — [Sponsored Story Ads](https://offers.theresandiego.com/SponsoredStories-page)
- **Newsletter ads:** "Single placement" is one ad slot in one weekly newsletter, and each edition goes out twice. The "Three-pack" is placements across three weekly newsletters at a bundled discount. The "Dedicated email" goes to subscribers with only that advertiser's content, also sent twice. — [Packages](https://theresandiego.com/packages/); [Newsletter Ads](https://offers.theresandiego.com/newsletter-ads-page)
- **Social Ads (Instagram/Facebook), three formats:**
  - (a) A "native" Instagram post ("not a promotional offer or 'ad'"), with one year on the Instagram feed guaranteed.
  - (b) A promotional-offer post.
  - (c) A giveaway contest: an Instagram post with a tag-and-follow giveaway live for 7 days, a link in bio, three Instagram Story features and a share to the Facebook page.

  — [Social Ads](https://offers.theresandiego.com/SocialAds-page)
- **Deal Ads:** tiered monthly deal listings. The entry tier shows the deal only on ThereSanDiego.com and targets customers within 1–2 miles. Higher tiers add Instagram/Facebook Stories, the newsletter, and paid "ad campaigns" (top tier only). A free deal listing is also mentioned. — [Deal Ads](https://offers.theresandiego.com/get-deals-page); [Advertise](https://advertise.theresandiego.com/)
- **Event Boosts:** impression-based promotion of events, tiered by impressions and radius. The entry "Crowd" tier is for hyper-local events, and the top tier is called "Mob" on the packages page. — [Event Boost Ads](https://offers.theresandiego.com/EventBoosts-page); [Packages](https://theresandiego.com/packages/)
- **Business Profile:** a permanent listing in the site's local guide, for a one-time fee. — [Advertise](https://advertise.theresandiego.com/)
- **Restaurant Packages:** a monthly bundle with one sponsored story plus targeted impressions ("Approximately 15k targeted impressions (geo, age & demo targeting)" and a ~50k tier). Ad campaign management and organic impressions are not charged. Single social posts go out on Facebook, Instagram and Twitter. — [Restaurant Packages](https://theresandiego.com/restaurant-packages/)
- **Editorial products that double as ad inventory:** "Best Restaurants in San Diego (2026 Guide)", "New Restaurants Opening in San Diego in 2026 (Updated Monthly)", "The Craving" weekly food column, and neighborhood guides. — [Best Restaurants](https://theresandiego.com/best-restaurants-in-san-diego/); [New Restaurants 2026](https://theresandiego.com/new-restaurants-opening-in-san-diego-in-2026/); [The Craving](https://theresandiego.com/the-craving-san-diego-restaurants-and-bars/)

### Inferences
- The "offers." subdomain, with page names like "-page" and visible booking dates (for example "Fri, Mar 24, 2023"), suggests a funnel or calendar-booking builder (GoHighLevel-style). That fits a "pick a package, book or pay online" model with little sales-rep involvement.
- The core value proposition is guaranteed paid-social impressions bundled with content. The publisher effectively resells boosted Meta ads on a branded article, with a margin above ad spend.

### Gaps
- What the "Deals - Learn More" pages (/stories, /social) contain beyond titles.
- Whether display or banner ads, affiliate links or ticketing revenue exist. None turned up in search.

**Newsletter:**
- Single $497, three-pack $997, dedicated email $1,500. — [Advertise](https://advertise.theresandiego.com/) / [Packages](https://theresandiego.com/packages/) (the search summary attributed these to the "main advertising page")
- **Stale (Pandemic Pricing):** single $397, triple $750, dedicated $1,500. — [Packages](https://theresandiego.com/packages/)

**Social Ads (page shows availability "Fri, Mar 24, 2023"):** native post, promo-offer post and giveaway contest are each $497 for one, $997 for three or $2,997 for twelve. That is about $249 per post at the 12-pack rate. — [Social Ads](https://offers.theresandiego.com/SocialAds-page)
- **Stale:** single social post (Facebook/Instagram/Twitter) $297 each or three for $750. — [Restaurant Packages](https://theresandiego.com/restaurant-packages/)

**Deal Ads (page references October 2022 dates):**
| Price/mo | Guarantee | Radius | Distribution |
|---|---|---|---|

| $497 | 20,000 impressions | (wider) | + paid ad campaigns |

— [Deal Ads](https://offers.theresandiego.com/get-deals-page)

**Event Boosts (October 2022 dates):** $97 = 5,000 impressions ("Crowd", hyper-local), $297 = 20,000 impressions, $497 = 50,000 impressions ("Mob"). — [Event Boost Ads](https://offers.theresandiego.com/EventBoosts-page); [Packages](https://theresandiego.com/packages/)

**Business Profile:** $297 one-time. **Deal listing:** free. — [Advertise](https://advertise.theresandiego.com/)

**Restaurant Packages (oldest; the search tool called the listing "about nine years old"):** a $497/month tier and a $697/month tier with one sponsored story plus about 15k or about 50k targeted impressions. Which price maps to which tier was unclear in the summary. A $997/month package with about 50k targeted impressions was also reported. — [Restaurant Packages](https://theresandiego.com/restaurant-packages/)

**Some packages show "Let's Chat"** in place of a price. — [Packages](https://theresandiego.com/packages/)

### Inferences
- The pricing ladder is consistent: $97 entry (hyper-local, 5k impressions), $297 (20k), $497 (core), $997 (3-pack or citywide), $1,500 (dedicated email or countywide). This is a clean template for a Saratoga replication. Saratoga is a far smaller market, so it would probably need lower impression guarantees and prices.
- Implied CPM on sponsored stories: about $50 at $497/10k, about $40 at $997/25k and about $30 at $1,500/50k, with the content included. This is calculated from the cited figures, not published.

### Gaps
- Exact live prices as of October 2026 could not be verified, because the pages were unreachable.
- Whether the $497/$997/$1,500 newsletter prices are post-pandemic replacements for $397/$750/$1,500. This is likely but unconfirmed.
- Discounts, contract terms, refund policy, and what happens when guarantees are missed.

## 3. Audience claims

### Takeaway
The company claims more than 70,000 monthly unique web visitors and "700,000+" monthly reach across web, email and social. It claims about 40K Facebook fans and about 42K Instagram followers. Its newsletter subscriber count varies by page (about 20K, 25K or nearly 30K). The audience skews 76% female and 75% aged 25–44. The newsletter goes out twice weekly (Tuesdays and Thursdays). No open rate was found.

### Cited Findings
- "Every month, over 70,000 unique visitors come to ThereSanDiego.com, and our content reaches 700,000+ people across web, email, and social." — [Advertise](https://theresandiego.com/advertise/); [Advertise hub](https://advertise.theresandiego.com/)
- Audience box: 40K fans (Facebook), 42K followers (Instagram), 25K subscribers, 700,000+ monthly reach. — [Advertise hub](https://advertise.theresandiego.com/)
- **Newsletter subscriber claims (contradictory):**
  - "nearly 20,000 highly-engaged subscribers" on the older page. — [Packages](https://theresandiego.com/packages/)
  - "over 25,000" — [Advertise](https://theresandiego.com/advertise/)
  - "nearly 30,000+" — [Newsletter Ads](https://offers.theresandiego.com/newsletter-ads-page)
  - 25,000 newsletter subscribers. — [Best Restaurants 2026](https://theresandiego.com/best-restaurants-in-san-diego/)
- **Web readers (contradictory):** the restaurant guide cites "40,000–80,000 monthly readers," against the 70,000+ uniques claim. — [Best Restaurants 2026](https://theresandiego.com/best-restaurants-in-san-diego/)
- **Cadence:** the newsletter "lands in your inbox twice a week with the best events, openings, and things to do" (Tuesdays and Thursdays). Newsletter ad placements run in a "weekly newsletter" that is sent twice. — [About](https://theresandiego.com/about/); [Packages](https://theresandiego.com/packages/)
- **Demographics:** 76% female and 75% aged 25–44. A "college-educated 37%/63%" figure appears but its label is ambiguous. — [Advertise](https://theresandiego.com/advertise/)
- **Social:** Instagram and Facebook profiles show roughly 41K–42K followers each. — [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/theresandiego/); [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/theresandiego/)
- San Diego Magazine (date unknown) credited creator Mike Cooch with "35,000-plus followers" on @ThereSanDiego. — [San Diego Magazine](https://sandiegomagazine.com/food-drink/san-diego-food-influencers-to-follow-right-now/)
- **Open rate:** none published on any ThereSanDiego page found. Benchmarks seen in the same search, from other outlets: a comparable SD publisher reported a 37% average open rate, and San Diego Magazine's 2021 kit listed 16% for one newsletter. — [SanDiegoVille media kit](https://www.sandiegoville.com/p/advertise.html); [SD Magazine 2021 kit](https://online.flippingbook.com/view/1062094137)

### Inferences
- The subscriber numbers probably reflect growth over time (about 20K, then 25K, then about 30K). The newsletter offer page (nearly 30K) is likely the newest figure, but this is unconfirmed.
- "700,000+ reach" is roughly 10 times the uniques. It is a self-reported aggregate, probably summing social impressions and email sends, and is unaudited.

### Gaps
- No third-party traffic verification (Similarweb, Quantcast) was found.
- No open or click rates, no income or geographic split, no date on the demographics.
