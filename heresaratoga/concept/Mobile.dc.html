The backyard

The backyard is the soul of Saratoga. Picnic tables under the trees, coolers, TVs everywhere and the paddock a few steps away. Get there when the gates open to claim a table.

Local tip
Go for the morning workouts. Coffee, mist on the track and almost nobody else there.
