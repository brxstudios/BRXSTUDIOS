Every summer, Saratoga Springs becomes the center of the horse racing universe. The race course has been running since 1863, which makes it one of the oldest sporting venues in the country, and for roughly six weeks from mid-July to Labor Day the whole city moves to its rhythm. Restaurants fill, hotel prices double, and Union Avenue turns into a parade of sundresses, seersucker and binoculars.

Here’s how to do it right.

## The basics

**When:** The summer meet traditionally runs from mid-July through Labor Day. NYRA has said it plans a 40-day meet for 2027, with dates usually announced early in the year. Check [NYRA’s Saratoga page](https://www.nyra.com/saratoga/) for the calendar, post times and dark days.

**Where:** Saratoga Race Course is on Union Avenue, an easy walk or bike ride from downtown Broadway.

**Tickets:** General admission gets you into the grandstand and backyard. Reserved grandstand seats, clubhouse seats and hospitality packages sell out fast for big days — buy as early as you can.

## Pick your experience

### The backyard

The backyard is the soul of Saratoga. Picnic tables under the trees, families with coolers, TVs everywhere and the paddock a few steps away, where you can see the horses up close before every race. Get there when the gates open to claim a table. NYRA’s rules on coolers, chairs and glass change from year to year, so check before you pack.

### The grandstand and clubhouse

If you want to watch the finish line, book a reserved seat. The clubhouse has a dress code — think collared shirts and summer dresses — and it’s worth looking the part even in the backyard. Saratoga is a place where people still wear hats to the races.

### Mornings at the track

The best-kept secret is the morning. Get up early, grab a coffee and watch the horses work out as the mist lifts off the track. Ask NYRA what morning programs are running this season — it’s one of the most beautiful things you can do in Saratoga, and it costs little or nothing.

## The Travers Stakes

The Travers was first run in 1864 and is the oldest major stakes race for three-year-olds in America. They call it the **Midsummer Derby**, and Travers Day — traditionally a Saturday in late August — is the biggest, loudest, best-dressed day of the season. Expect huge crowds. Arrive early, plan your ride home, and book dinner reservations weeks ahead.

## The Graveyard of Champions

Saratoga has a reputation for upsets. Man o’ War lost only one race in his life, here, in 1919, to a horse named — really — Upset. Secretariat lost the Whitney here in 1973. American Pharoah, fresh off the Triple Crown, was beaten in the 2015 Travers. Bet accordingly.

## Track season tips

- **Book your stay early.** Months early, especially for Travers weekend. See our [where to stay](/explore/stay/) list.
- **Walk or bike.** Traffic and parking around Union Avenue are tough on big days.
- **Eat off-hours.** Restaurants on Broadway are slammed from 6 to 9pm. Reserve, or go early.
- **Visit the museum.** The [National Museum of Racing and Hall of Fame](https://www.racingmuseum.org/) is right across the street.
- **Don’t skip downtown at night.** After the last race, Broadway and Caroline Street are where the party continues.
