The Saratoga Performing Arts Center — everyone calls it SPAC — sits in the pines of Saratoga Spa State Park. It opened in 1966 and is the summer home of the **New York City Ballet** and **The Philadelphia Orchestra**, and it also hosts many of the biggest concerts in upstate New York. A summer night here is one of the best things you can do in Saratoga.

## Seats or lawn?

The covered amphitheater has reserved seats, and behind it is a huge sloping lawn. Lawn tickets are cheaper, the atmosphere is better and you can bring a picnic. Seats are worth it for ballet, where you’ll want to see the dancers’ feet.

## What to bring to the lawn

- A blanket or low beach chairs (check SPAC’s current rules on chair height)
- A picnic — this is half the fun
- Bug spray and a layer for after sunset
- Rain gear, just in case

SPAC’s policies on outside food, drink and bag sizes differ between classical performances and the big concerts, so check [spac.org](https://www.spac.org/) before you pack.

## The season

- **Spring and early summer:** Concert season ramps up and the Saratoga Jazz Festival takes over a weekend in late June.
- **July:** The New York City Ballet residency.
- **August:** The Philadelphia Orchestra.
- **All summer:** Rock, pop and country tours.

## Getting there and away

SPAC is about a ten-minute drive south of downtown. For big concerts, parking and leaving can take a while — arrive early, or bike or rideshare. If you’re staying downtown, an Uber back to Broadway beats an hour in the parking lot.

## Make a night of it

Get there early and walk Spa State Park’s trails or the Geyser Creek springs before the show. Our [Spa State Park guide](/guides/saratoga-spa-state-park/) has the route. Staying the night? The **Gideon Putnam** hotel is a short walk from the gates.
