Saratoga Spa State Park is the biggest reason to drive five minutes south of downtown. It’s a National Historic Landmark with more than 2,000 acres of pine forest, naturally carbonated springs and grand neoclassical buildings from the 1930s, when New York State built it as a European-style health spa.

## Don’t miss

### Geyser Creek and the Island Spouter

Follow the trail along Geyser Creek to the **Island Spouter**, a spring that sprays mineral water from a crusted orange mound. It’s one of the most photographed spots in Saratoga.

### The springs

Fill a bottle at **State Seal Spring**, the mildest and most drinkable water in town. See our [guide to drinking the springs](/guides/drinking-the-springs/).

### The bath houses

The **Roosevelt Baths & Spa** still offers mineral baths. The **Lincoln Baths** and the **Hall of Springs** are worth a walk past for the architecture alone.

### Museums

The **Saratoga Automobile Museum** is in the park’s former bottling plant, and the **National Museum of Dance** is just across South Broadway.

## Things to do by season

**Summer:** Swim at the park’s historic pools, play golf, catch a show at [SPAC](/guides/spac/) and picnic under the pines.

**Fall:** The best time for the trails. Long walks, cool air and changing leaves.

**Winter:** Cross-country skiing and snowshoeing on the trails when there’s snow, then a hot mineral bath.

**Spring:** Quiet trails and the first concerts of the season.

## Where to stay

The **Gideon Putnam**, built as part of the original spa, sits in the middle of the park. It’s the best base for a SPAC night.

## Know before you go

There’s a vehicle use fee at some times of year and lots of free parking around the park. Check the New York State Parks website for current hours, pool season and fees.
