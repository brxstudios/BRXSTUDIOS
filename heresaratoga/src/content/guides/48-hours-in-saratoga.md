Saratoga Springs is small enough to walk and packed with enough to fill a week. If you have a weekend, this is how we’d spend it.

## Day one: downtown

### Morning — coffee and Congress Park

Start with a pastry and coffee at [Mrs. London’s](/explore/eat-drink/) on Broadway, then walk into **Congress Park**. This is where Saratoga began as a resort town: duck ponds, statues, the historic carousel and **Congress Spring**, where you can taste the naturally carbonated mineral water the city was built on. (Read our [guide to drinking the springs](/guides/drinking-the-springs/) first.)

### Late morning — the Canfield Casino

In the middle of the park sits the **Canfield Casino**, the 1870s gambling hall that drew the Gilded Age’s richest players. It’s now home to the Saratoga Springs History Museum.

### Afternoon — Union Avenue

Walk east on Union Avenue, past the grand Victorian houses, to the **National Museum of Racing and Hall of Fame**. Then continue to **Yaddo Gardens**, the rose garden of the famous artists’ retreat, which is open to the public. In summer, this is where you’d spend the afternoon at the **Saratoga Race Course** instead — see our [track season guide](/guides/track-season/).

### Evening — dinner and a show

Have dinner at **Hattie’s**, serving Southern fried chicken on Phila Street since 1938. Then walk next door to **Caffè Lena**, open since 1960 and one of the oldest folk music coffeehouses in America — a young Bob Dylan played here. Check the schedule and book ahead; the room is small.

### Late — Caroline Street

Finish on Caroline Street, Saratoga’s bar block. If you’re still going at the end of the night, it’s a local tradition to end it with a doughboy at **Esperanto**.

## Day two: the park and beyond

### Morning — Saratoga Spa State Park

Drive or bike south on Broadway to **Saratoga Spa State Park**. Walk the Geyser Creek trail to the Island Spouter, a mineral spring that shoots out of an orange, crusted mound, then wander past the classical bath houses and the Hall of Springs. Read our [Spa State Park guide](/guides/saratoga-spa-state-park/).

### Midday — a mineral bath

Book a soak at the **Roosevelt Baths & Spa** in the park, where you can still bathe in Saratoga’s mineral water.

### Afternoon — pick one

- **History:** Drive 25 minutes to **Saratoga National Historical Park**, where the Battles of Saratoga turned the Revolutionary War in 1777.
- **Water:** Head to **Saratoga Lake** for lunch by the water or a paddle.
- **Shopping:** Browse Broadway, starting at **Northshire Bookstore**.

### Evening — SPAC or Broadway

In summer, end with a show at **SPAC**: the New York City Ballet, the Philadelphia Orchestra or a big-name concert, with a picnic on the lawn. See our [SPAC guide](/guides/spac/). The rest of the year, book dinner on Broadway and catch whatever’s on the [events calendar](/events/).

## Getting here

Saratoga Springs is about three and a half hours north of New York City by car, and Amtrak stops in town. Albany International Airport is roughly half an hour away.
